<p align="center"><b>Know who is really on the other side of the request.</b></p>

Trace is open-source, self-hosted device identification and fraud signals. It recognises returning devices across cleared cookies, private windows and browser switches, and scores each visit for bots, VPNs, tampering and shared or farmed accounts. Every score comes with the reasons behind it, and your data stays on your own infrastructure.

### Start here

- **[trace](https://github.com/trace-id/trace)**: the server, dashboard, SDKs and documentation
- **Browser and framework SDKs**: JavaScript, React, Next.js, Vue, Nuxt, Svelte, Angular, Solid
- **Mobile SDKs**: React Native and Expo, Flutter, Kotlin, Swift (Swift and parts of Kotlin and Flutter are early drafts)
- **Server SDKs**: Node.js, Python, PHP, Go, Java, Ruby, .NET (Ruby and .NET are early drafts), with framework guards for Express, Fastify, Django, Flask, FastAPI and Laravel
- **Edge proxies**: Cloudflare, Netlify, Vercel and Fastly, for first-party mode

### Principles

- Open source and self-hosted by default
- Scores always come with reasons
- Browser collectors never prompt, never make network requests and never read devices, files or location
- Accuracy numbers are labelled as synthetic unless measured on real traffic
